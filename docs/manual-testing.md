# Manual testing

Questions to paste into the chat, taken from the [evaluation sets](recommender/README.md#evaluation).
For recommendations, check that the answer gives the requested number of titles and that each one fits.

1. Genre recommendation. Expected: 3 fantasy anime

   ```text
   Recommend three anime in the fantasy genre.
   ```

2. Main characters. Expected: Saitama, Genos

   ```text
   Who is in One-Punch Man season one's main character cast?
   ```

3. Length recommendation. Expected: 3 TV anime with at most 12 episodes each

   ```text
   Recommend three TV anime with at most 12 episodes in the specific series you suggest.
   ```

4. Release year. Expected: 2013

   ```text
   What year did the first season of Attack on Titan start airing?
   ```

5. Tag recommendation. Expected: 3 anime with the Found Family tag

   ```text
   Recommend three anime with a found family theme.
   ```

6. Source material. Expected: original

   ```text
   Was Spirited Away adapted from a previously published work or made as an original anime?
   ```

7. Format recommendation. Expected: 5 anime films

   ```text
   Give me five anime made as films instead of episodic shows.
   ```

8. Main animation studio. Expected: MAPPA

   ```text
   Which animation studio made the first JUJUTSU KAISEN TV season?
   ```

9. Release year recommendation. Expected: 3 anime first released in 2020 or later

   ```text
   Recommend three anime first released in 2020 or later.
   ```

10. Format. Expected: TV

    ```text
    Is Angel Beats! listed as a TV series, movie, or ONA?
    ```

11. Tag recommendation. Expected: 3 anime with the Time Manipulation tag

    ```text
    Which three anime would you recommend for the Time Manipulation tag?
    ```

12. Main characters. Expected: Tanjirou Kamado, Nezuko Kamado, Inosuke Hashibira, Zenitsu Agatsuma

    ```text
    Can you list the main characters of Demon Slayer season one?
    ```

13. Genre recommendation. Expected: 3 horror anime

    ```text
    Give me three anime from the horror genre.
    ```

14. Release year. Expected: 2001

    ```text
    What year did Spirited Away come out?
    ```

15. Length recommendation. Expected: 3 TV anime or TV shorts with episodes of 15 minutes or less

    ```text
    Recommend three TV anime or TV shorts whose episodes run no more than 15 minutes each.
    ```

16. Main animation studio. Expected: Bones

    ```text
    What studio animated Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood?
    ```

17. Format recommendation. Expected: 4 TV series (not TV shorts)

    ```text
    What are four regular television anime series I could follow episode by episode?
    ```

18. Source material. Expected: light novel

    ```text
    What medium is credited as Toradora!'s source material?
    ```

19. Release year recommendation. Expected: 3 anime first released from 1990 to 1999

    ```text
    What are three anime released between 1990 and 1999, inclusive?
    ```

20. Format. Expected: TV

    ```text
    What is Solo Leveling's first anime season classified as: TV, movie, or OVA?
    ```
